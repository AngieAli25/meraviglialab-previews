About the Experience

Step back in time and discover the Amalfi Coast the way it was meant to be explored — slowly, beautifully, and with heart. Aboard a perfectly restored vintage Fiat, your guide Antonio will take you through winding coastal roads, hidden panoramic viewpoints, and centuries-old villages.

More than just a tour, this is a personal journey curated by someone who deeply loves this land and its stories.

Meet Antonio

Antonio is not just your driver — he is your storyteller, your local friend, and your personal photographer for the day. Born and raised near the Amalfi Coast, he brings an infectious passion for the region's history, culture, and hidden gems.

His warmth, knowledge, and genuine care for every guest make this experience truly one of a kind.

The Tour

Your adventure begins with a pickup directly from Amalfi. From there, Antonio guides you along the coastline, stopping at the most spectacular viewpoints for breathtaking photos and captivating stories.

Whether you are a couple seeking romance, a solo traveler craving authenticity, or a group of friends chasing the perfect shot — this tour is tailored to make your visit to the Amalfi Coast absolutely unforgettable.
